      01

      The pipeline

      Five stages turn 60,000 raw telemetry ticks into a ranked list of suspects — each stage maps to a project deliverable.

      1. Stage 1

        Data preparation

        Load tick-level telemetry, de-duplicate, sort by player & time, treat structural missing values.

      2. Stage 2

        Feature extraction

        Compress 300 ticks per player into a 10-dimensional behavioural fingerprint.

      3. Stage 3

        DBSCAN clustering

        Standardise features, pick ε from the k-distance knee, find dense regions of normal play.

      4. Stage 4

        Anomaly scoring

        Score = distance to the nearest normal core point ÷ ε. Above 1 means outside normal behaviour.

      5. Stage 5

        Evaluation

        Reveal the hidden labels only now — precision, recall, F1 and a confusion matrix.

      02

      Dataset & preprocessing

      Simulated multiplayer telemetry sampled at 10 Hz. Every player has a personality — a skill level, reaction speed and aim steadiness — so some honest pros look suspicious, and some cheaters are subtle.

      Behavioural fingerprint

      04

      Density-based detection

      DBSCAN groups players who sit in dense neighbourhoods. Honest players are many and similar, so they form a dense core. Cheaters are few and strange, so they fall out as noise — or form tiny micro-clusters when they share the same cheat tool.

      Choosing ε — k-distance graph

      Each player's distance to its 5th nearest neighbour, sorted. The knee (point of maximum curvature) is where density drops off — that distance becomes ε.

      Benchmark · DBSCAN vs other detectorssame features · same data

      Every other method must be told the cheat rate in advance (its contamination parameter). In real life nobody knows that number, so each one is tested with a low 5%, a near-correct 12% and a high 20% guess. DBSCAN needs no guess at all.

      Conclusion

      ✦

      Conclusion

      △

      Limitations

      • Synthetic telemetry — real games add lag, map geometry and team play.
      • A single global ε assumes one density; mixed skill brackets may need several.
      • Elite human players sit close to the boundary and can be flagged.
      • Batch analysis — scores are computed after a match, not live.
      ➜

      Future improvements

      • HDBSCAN for variable density across ranks and regions.
      • Sequence features (LSTM embeddings) of aim trajectories.
      • Streaming scoring during live matches with a review queue.
      • Validation on real, labelled anti-cheat datasets.
